## Hardware Pin Map (quick reference)

- **Clocks**:8MHz HXTAL

### ADC
- PA0: ADC0/1/2_IN0(ADC_A)
- PA1: ADC0/1/2_IN1(ADC_B)
- PA3: ADC0/1/2_IN3(ADC_POW)

### USART
- PB6: USART0_TX
- PB7: USART0_RX

### I2C(磁编码器)
- PB8: I2C0_SCL
- PB9: I2C0_SDA

### PWM (TIMER0 complementary)
- PA8: TIMER0_CH0(PWMA)
- PA9: TIMER0_CH1(PWMB)
- PA10: TIMER0_CH2(PWMC)
- PB13: TIMER0_CH0_N(PWMAN)
- PB14: TIMER0_CH1_N(PWMBN)
- PB15: TIMER0_CH2_N(PWMCN)

### LEDs (GPIO out)
- PB1: LEDR
- PB2: LEDB
- PA7: LEDG

## 其他预留外设，暂不实现
### CAN0
- PA12 CAN0_TX
- PA11 CAN0_RX

### 霍尔传感器接口
- PB4:HALL_U(TIMER2_CH0)
- PB5:HALL_V(TIMER2_CH1)
- PB0:HALL_W(TIMER2_CH2)

### 备用串口
- PB10: USART2_TX
- PB11: USART2_RX

### 不使用的第三相ADC
- PA2: ADC0/1/2_IN2(ADC_C)
